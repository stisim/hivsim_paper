## Decisions for Robyn

1. **Condom fix:** its own STIsim PR into v4, or added to #612?
2. **On-ART mortality:** PR it (after #612)? Which EMOD row is the table's reference: men 25–35 (now) or under 25? Should HIVsim Malawi use it (with CD4 eligibility and testing for advanced HIV, per analysis 13 §4)?
3. **Engine CRN fix:** add to #1573, or a separate PR for Cliff? Rerun of analysis 14 with both fixes (60 seeds; `/tmp/agents-nov09/P/compare.txt`, not committed): paired runs stay identical in infections, deaths and partnerships until 2004.5 (was 1991.2), and VRFs are large to 2010 (PLHIV 300 in 2005), but 2020–24 outputs gain at most 10–20% in variance. So it is worth merging for correctness, not for cheaper headline estimates. The condom fix changes the seed counts far more, through the epidemic: Δ new infections ≈ +160% (30–40 seeds), Δ PLHIV ≈ −10% (160–360 seeds), share of deaths on ART ~125 seeds.
4. **Close #607 and #609** in favour of #612?
5. **Seeding on the path:** EMOD's seeding on HIVsim's demography takes off in 2–3 of 5 runs at 10,000 agents. Condition on take-off, make seeding its own step, or seed more?
6. **HIVsim reference sets:** with the condom fix, does experiment 03's set go into `models/hivsim/reference/` provisionally, or does calibration resume first (with the new mortality)?
