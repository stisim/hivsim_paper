# 10: EMOD′ and Optima′ on Starsim 4.0.0rc3

**Question:** moving the rebuilt models from Starsim v4 at ebd52c1a (4.0.0rc2-era, what analyses 05–08 used) to Starsim 4.0.0rc3 (PyPI), does anything change beyond what rc3 changes by design? By design (Starsim's CHANGELOG, 4.0.0, and `docs/migration/v4_changelog.md`):
- A step is taken from every time point, the last included, and the first step is `ti == 0` (it was 1). A stock at row `ti` is the state at the start of step `ti`, and a flow the total over it (a flow was over the step ending at `ti`). For the declared Optima′, "the stocks are as before at every time point and each flow at row `ti` is the one that was at `ti + 1`". For EMOD′, written as code, every step reads the same time as before, but there is one more step, from the start: step `k` now reads `t0 + k·dt` after `k` earlier steps, as EMOD's own loop does (`Simulation::Update` updates at `currentTime`, then increments it), where at ebd52c1a it came after `k − 1`.
- As agents, draws are keyed by stream, step and round, so EMOD′ agrees with the earlier runs only in distribution.
- Renames and API changes (migration only, no change in results): `update_results()` split into `record_stocks()` and `record_flows()`; a delivery's trigger is `when=` (events, `ss.on(..., delay=)`, times or rounds) and `who=` only says who is eligible; `deliver=` is gone (a function `who=` with rounds at every step); `ss.ppl` → `ss.person`, `ss.null` → `ss.unborn`, `use_aging=` → `aging=`, `ss.timeseries(mode=)` → `interpolation=`, `ss.transfer(dict(pop=...))`, `sim.pars.pop_scale` → `sim.pop_scale`, a module's `on_death()`.

**What's run:**
- Versions: Starsim 4.0.0rc3 from PyPI (with its Rust kernels; env `hp_rc3`), against the saved outputs of analyses 04, 06 and 08 (Starsim ebd52c1a). Optima′'s acceptance is also rerun on ebd52c1a (`/tmp/starsim-ebd52c1a` on `PYTHONPATH`, env `hivsim_paper`) for the before column.
- Optima′ (deterministic ODE): `models/optima/v4/acceptance.py` on each version, outputs `optima_acceptance_{ebd52c1a,rc3}.txt`. Expected: stocks identical; flows identical once `compare.py` reads the flow at the whole year's row rather than the next.
- EMOD′ (agents): analysis 06's runs (compiled campaign and condensed cascade, 10 sets × 3 seeds, the same seeds) and analysis 04's (the interpreted campaign, the same 10 × 3), on rc3: 90 runs, `run.py emod`. Each is compared with its ebd52c1a runs saved in 04 and 06: target points (`emod_targets.measure()`), z = (rc3 median − ebd52c1a median)/SE with SE from both sets of 30 runs, as 06 did, and the cascade over time. 06's own comparison (condensed vs compiled) and 04's (EMOD′ vs EMOD, `raw_results/emod/sub10`) are redone on rc3.
- Run time: the same EMOD′ runs (condensed and compiled, set 0) on both versions, side by side at the same load, `run.py time` in each environment.
- Optima′ in analysis 08 (ii): its three runs (own cascade, EMOD's cascade as rates, without VMMC and PrEP) on rc3, `run.py optima`, against `../08_optima_condensed_cascade/outputs/`.
- `run.py compare` writes the comparison tables; `run.py --plot` the figures.

**How results compare:** Optima′ number for number. EMOD′ in distribution: with 30 runs per model, a target point whose z is beyond ±2 in more than about 5% of points, or a systematic shift in a measure across points, would mean a change beyond the draws. The one-step shift in when EMOD′'s steps start is expected to be too small to see (a month in 65 years).
