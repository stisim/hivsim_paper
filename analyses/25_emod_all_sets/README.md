# 25: EMOD with both fixes, all 250 calibrated sets: where does 30,582 come from?

**Question:** analysis 18 ran 10 of Daniel's 250 resampled sets (× 5 seeds) with both PR #4 fixes. Its Figure 3A shares matched, but the 2024 total was 41,300 new infections (median; sets 30,600–60,500) against the Figure 3 caption's 30,582. Over all 250 sets: what is the distribution of 2024 totals, and does 30,582 correspond to (a) the mean/median of the 250, (b) the sets that fit best under the fixed model, or (c) the sets Daniel ranked best (his `likelihood` column, from his 2023 build with the PR #1 files)? Does the total correlate with fit, and does any group of sets give both the shares and the total?

**Runs (`launch.py`):** the 250 sets are 129 distinct parameter vectors (analysis 01). Each distinct vector runs once per seed, seeds 1 and 2 (258 runs), as analysis 18's "both fixes" variant: `run_emod.run_one` with `transmission=True`, to 2025, inputs = a copy of `source/InputFiles` with `Static/Demographics_Malawi.json` and `Templates/campaign_Malawi_20251215.json` from `origin/mwi_demo` (PR #4). The inputs this builds for set 0 seed 1 are identical to analysis 18's `fig3a_both/set000_s1` (up to the last digit of some floats). Seed-major job order, so every set gets seed 1 before any gets seed 2. Output: `raw_results/emod/a25_both`. `outputs/set_map.csv` maps each of the 250 sets (`parameterization_id`, `run_number`, `likelihood`) to the representative it was run as (`rep_pid`, the lowest id with the same vector). Results over "all 250" weight each distinct vector by its multiplicity.

**Measured per run (`run.py`):** as analysis 18: new infections at ages 15–64 from `ReportHIVByAgeAndGender`, × 17,563,749 / sim population at 2018.42 (Daniel's scaling, `mwi_transmission.Rmd`), 2024 annualised over the half-years present; 2020 and 2024 Figure 3A shares from `common/transmission.py`.

**Fit to EMOD's own calibration targets:** the ingest form (`source/Data/calibration_ingest_form_Malawi_3_node_DTC.xlsm`, sheets `Analyzers` and `Obs-*`): prevalence by age and sex (Beta, weight 0.8), ARTCoverage (Beta, 0.15), population 2018 by age and sex (Gaussian, 0.025, sim scaled to 8,258,624 aged 15–49 in 2018), number on ART 15–64 by sex (Gaussian, 0.025, scaled likewise); incidence has weight 0. dtk-tools' `HIVCalibSite` isn't available, so the score is an approximation: Beta channels as the log density of the sim proportion under Beta(eff·p + 1, eff·(1 − p) + 1), Gaussian channels as Normal log density with sd = two_sigma / 2, each channel's mean over its points, weighted sum. Checked against Daniel's `likelihood` on analysis 01's PR #1-file runs (`raw_results/emod/all250`): the score is only usable as "his likelihood under the fixed model" to the extent those correlate. The fixed-model score is the mean over the two seeds.
